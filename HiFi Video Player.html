📄 Privacy Policy

HiFi Video Player

Last updated: April 21, 2026

1. Introduction

HiFi Video Player ("the App") is a local video playback application designed to provide high-quality media playback without relying on cloud services or user accounts.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data. By using the App, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Data Collection

HiFi Video Player is designed with privacy in mind.

We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal data.

Specifically:

  • No account registration is required
  • No personal information is collected
  • No analytics or tracking services are used
  • No user data is transmitted to any server

All media playback happens locally on your device or through direct connections you initiate (such as SFTP or streaming URLs).

3. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to function properly:

📁 Storage Access

Used to: Read and play local video and audio files on your device

🌐 Network Access

Used to:

  • Stream online media (e.g., HTTP, HLS streams)
  • Connect to remote servers (e.g., SFTP playback)

The App does not use network access to collect or transmit personal data.

🔌 USB Access (if applicable)

Used to: Detect and communicate with external USB DAC devices for high-quality audio output

No data from USB devices is collected or transmitted.

4. Data Usage

Since no personal data is collected:

  • No data is stored
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • No data is used for advertising or analytics

All operations remain on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

HiFi Video Player does not integrate any third-party services that collect user data, such as:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations

6. Remote Connections

The App allows users to connect to remote sources such as:

  • SFTP servers
  • Streaming media URLs

These connections are initiated and configured entirely by the user. The App does not monitor, log, or transmit this connection data.

7. Children's Privacy

HiFi Video Player is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Security

Because the App does not collect or store personal data, there is minimal risk of data exposure.

All media playback and processing occur locally on the device or through user-initiated connections.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
